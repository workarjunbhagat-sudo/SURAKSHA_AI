SURAKSHA AI v8 — Voice Demo

Recommended demo:
1. Select हिंदी.
2. Ask: "कोई मुझसे OTP मांग रहा है, मैं क्या करूं?"
3. Enter the recognized text in the confirmation box.
4. Click GET FEMALE VOICE ANSWER.
5. Play the generated audio — Hindi female neural voice, slowed for senior-friendly clarity.

English:
"Someone is asking me for an OTP. What should I do?"

Hinglish:
"Koi mujhse OTP maang raha hai, main kya karun?"

Internet is required for neural voice generation. If it is unavailable, SURAKSHA falls back to the browser voice.
